How to read this table Current is a level from 1 to 5, derived from the measured compliance of the controls in that domain — not from an opinion. A domain scoring 60–84% of its controls sits at level 3 (Managed), 85–94% at level 4, 95% and above at level 5; the definitions are above. Target is one level up, the next realistic step rather than perfection, and Gap is the distance between them. The key action column states the measurement the level came from, so every figure can be traced back to the control tables earlier in this report.

A level is an average, and an average can hide a single control that matters more than the rest. Where that happens the key action names the weakest controls explicitly — read those before reading the level.
